Scam Check

Contract Check

Flags likely invalid clauses in plain language before she signs. So a contract she can’t read yet can’t trap her.

Evidence Pack

Judged by
What I learned

Build the system first

On Day 1, the design system (colors, type and components) was set up before any screen. When the brief changed, we didn’t start over. The landlord side came together from parts we already had.

Agree on the why

Before drawing anything, we agreed on one rule: judge facts, never people. Whenever we got stuck, we asked one question: does this follow the rule?

The story sells it

The jury had seven minutes with our app. So the deck followed Priya and Sabine step by step, instead of listing features.
